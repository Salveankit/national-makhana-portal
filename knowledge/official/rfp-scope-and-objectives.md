# RFP Scope And Objectives

The National Makhana Board request for proposal seeks design, development, and management of a web portal for a period of two years. The intent is not only to create a public website, but to provide an integrated portal and dashboard for workflow management, monitoring, and decision support.

Core scope areas include:

- Development of a website and integrated dashboard
- State-wise login modules for authority, board, and participating states
- Beneficiary registration and application submission workflows
- Possible third-party API integrations
- Operations and maintenance

The dashboard is expected to provide real-time or regularly updated visibility into cultivation area, farmers covered, infrastructure, production, Annual Action Plan progress, applications, and scheme implementation support.

The portal should act as a single-window platform for registration, application receipt, verification, clarification handling, inspection, approval, monitoring, and communication. Applicants may receive updates through SMS, email, and WhatsApp when those services are integrated.

The project emphasizes role-based workflows, transparency, accountability, auditability, and support for future integration with systems such as LGD, Agristack, and communication gateways.
