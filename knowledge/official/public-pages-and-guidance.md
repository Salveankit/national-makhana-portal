# Public Pages And Guidance

The public-facing portal is intended to reduce confusion and quickly route users to the right service path.

Home page purpose:

- establishes official government identity
- explains that the portal covers beneficiary services, officer workflows, planning, and monitoring
- signals that this is a single-window access point rather than a simple informational microsite

Services page purpose:

- presents the digital service catalogue
- groups services by beneficiaries, inspectors, officers, and administrators
- helps users map a task to the correct workspace

Schemes page purpose:

- explains intervention areas such as cultivation support, processing and value addition, training and extension, inspection and verification, and state planning
- sets user expectations that the portal supports implementation workflows beyond scheme reading

Updates page purpose:

- carries circulars, advisories, service notices, and operational announcements
- frames the portal as an official communication surface

Helpdesk page purpose:

- provides quick assistance for registration, application tracking, login support, and officer access
- reduces exposure to internal terminology by using guided user-facing language

Public guidance rules for chatbot responses:

- direct users to secure login for role-based actions
- avoid exposing internal-only workflow terms unless also explained plainly
- use formal, government-facing wording
- prefer concrete next steps over generic replies
